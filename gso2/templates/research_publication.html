Welcome to the Journal of Global South Quarterly. This section features peer-reviewed academic papers, rigorous research, and comprehensive studies focusing on international relations, geopolitics, security, and socio-economic developments across the Global South.

We invite academicians and researchers to submit their original papers for our upcoming quarterly issue. Submissions must adhere to our strict academic guidelines and peer-review process.
