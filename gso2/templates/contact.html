Write for Us

Global South Observer (GSO) invites academicians, researchers, civil servants, industry experts, and informed citizens to contribute their perspectives, research findings, and analysis to our platform. We publish scholarly articles, commentaries, opinion pieces, book reviews, and policy analyses on all aspects of Global South affairs.

What We Publish

  • Research articles on international relations, geopolitics, security, military strategic affairs
  • Modern warfare & defence technologies analysis
  • Public policy & world governance commentary
  • SDGs, philosophy, science and entertainment pieces
  • Healthcare, biodiversity, blue economy and clean energy articles
  • Book reviews and critical analyses

Submission Guidelines

  • Articles should be 1200 to 2000 words, line space 1.5, Times New Roman font
  • Include an 80-word author bio and passport-size photo
  • All claims must have references, bibliographies and hyperlinked citations
  • Must be original — no AI-generated content permitted
  • Must be free from plagiarism and comply with copyright laws
  • Submit as a Word document to: contactglobalsouthobserver@gmail.com
