Research Archived

Our Research Archive contains all published articles, commentaries, opinions, book reviews, and policy briefs produced by GSO researchers and contributors since our founding. Subscribers gain full access to this growing repository of scholarship on Global South affairs.

Research Articles

Peer-reviewed and editorially reviewed research articles on geopolitics, security, diplomacy, and governance.

Commentaries & Opinions

Expert opinions, commentaries, and analyses on current events affecting the Global South region.

Book Reviews

Critical reviews of important academic works on international relations, foreign policy, and development.

Journal of Global South Quarterly

The Journal of Global South Quarterly is GSO's flagship academic publication, featuring original research, policy analysis, and critical perspectives on developments across the Global South. Published quarterly, the Journal serves as a premier outlet for scholarship on issues affecting developing nations worldwide.

Journal Scope

International relations & modern diplomacy, geopolitics & security, defense & modern warfare, public policy & world governance, artificial intelligence & economy, environment & sustainable development, philosophy & science, literature & entertainment, healthcare, biodiversity, and blue economy.

To submit a paper for consideration in the Journal, please follow our editorial guidelines and send your manuscript to contactglobalsouthobserver@gmail.com.

New Policy Documents

Subscribers receive access to new policy documents, briefing papers, and working papers produced by the GSO research team. These documents provide in-depth analysis and recommendations on key policy challenges facing Global South nations.

Policy Briefs

Concise policy-focused documents providing analysis and recommendations on specific issues.

Working Papers

Preliminary research findings and analysis shared for discussion and comment before final publication.

Research Reports

Comprehensive research reports on specific topics, regions, or policy areas of concern to the Global South.

Annual Reports

The Global South Observer Annual Report provides a comprehensive overview of our activities, research outputs, programme highlights, partnerships, and impact over the preceding year. Annual Reports are available to all subscribers and provide transparency on our work and direction.

Annual Report Contents

Research highlights and publications, programme summaries (internships, leadership, seminars), partnership updates, financial transparency (where applicable), team updates, plans and priorities for the coming year.
