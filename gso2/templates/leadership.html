Founder Column

Global South Observer was established with a vision to create an independent, non-partisan platform for research, analysis, and dialogue on issues affecting the Global South. The founding team comprises distinguished academicians, retired civil servants, industrialists, and senior community members committed to advancing new narratives for the developing world.

Our founders believe that the Global South — encompassing more than 130 nations across Africa, Asia, Latin America, and Oceania — must have a robust, research-backed voice in global discourse. Through rigorous scholarship, evidence-based analysis, and inclusive dialogue, we seek to bridge the gap between academia and policy-making.

Founding Vision

To build a repository of shared knowledge and wisdom on the Global South region, promoting the research work of young researchers, early academicians, civil servants, entrepreneurs, and senior community members.

Founding Principle
"The Global South deserves a world-class platform that amplifies its voices, showcases its scholarship, and shapes global narratives from within."

— GSO Founding Team

Editorial Board

The Global South Observer Editorial Board comprises experienced academics, journalists, policy analysts, and subject-matter experts who oversee the quality, accuracy, and integrity of all published content. The editorial team ensures that all submissions meet our rigorous standards of scholarship, originality, and analytical depth.

Editorial Guidelines

The GSO seeks well-written scholarly work, commentaries, and opinions with critical and analytical perspectives on recent events in international relations, geopolitics, security, military strategic affairs, public policy, SDGs, philosophy, science, entertainment, healthcare, biodiversity, and more.

Submission Guidelines

  • Articles should be around 1200 to 2000 words, line space 1.5, writing style in Times New Roman, original, topical, and informed by primary qualitative and quantitative data.
  • Authors may forward their 80 words research affiliation and passport photo along with their separate article written in word file.
  • Pieces must be written in an analytical and critical style and include references, bibliographies and hyperlinked citations for all important claims.
  • GSO does not permit the use of generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) to assist with the content or writing. Ideas, writing, and citations must be your own.
  • Articles should comply with the copyright regulations of India and be free from plagiarism.
  • Submit to: contactglobalsouthobserver@gmail.com

Research Fellows

Global South Observer Research Fellows are accomplished scholars and practitioners who contribute to our research agenda through original publications, policy briefs, and collaborative projects. Fellows bring deep expertise from their respective regions and disciplines.

Senior Research Fellows

Experienced scholars contributing long-form research, policy analysis, and book reviews on Global South affairs.

Associate Research Fellows

Early-career academics and policy professionals contributing commentaries, news analysis, and opinion pieces.

Research Interns

Young scholars participating in our structured internship programme to develop research and analytical skills.
