Who We Are?

Global South Observer (GSO) is a web-based 'think-tank' founded under the guidelines of group of like-minded academicians, researcher, retired civil servants, Industrialist and senior community members to build a repository of shared knowledge and wisdom on global south region. GSO endeavors to promote the research work of young researchers, early academicians, civil servants, young entrepreneurs and senior community members by giving them the opportunity to publish their opinions, research articles, commentaries, news analysis, book reviews to building new narratives for global south region.

Our Core Focus Areas

International relations, geopolitics, security, military strategic affairs, modern warfare & defence technologies, public policy & world governance, SDGs, philosophy, science, entertainment, healthcare, biodiversity, blue economy, clean & nuclear energy.

What We Are Offering?

  • Professional Membership: We are offering 'Professional Membership' in our organization, where the team members will get opportunities to write with us and share their knowledge and wisdom for broader purposes.
  • Research Internships: The platform is offering the 'Research Internships' particularly for young scholars those who involved in beginning stage of their academic and research career.
  • Leadership Programs: The platform also provide the certificate based 'Leadership Programs' to trained the future leaders to perform well in various fields such as political, corporate and industry leadership, educational and community leadership programs and for various ground level NGOs.
  • Political Consultancy: Our team is also offering 'Political Consultancy' from village to national and global level leadership, inculcate democratic practices and values, expert advises to understand local challenges and providing platform for young scholars of developing nations of global south.
  • Subscription Based Access: We are also providing 'Subscription based access' to our readers to get updated Annual research reports, research archive, Journal/magazine access, newsletters and policy documents access related to global south countries.
  • Seminar & Webinar Sessions: Global South Observer also organizes 'Seminar & Webinar Sessions, Interviews, Podcasts' on various recent issues related to international relations, geopolitics, foreign policy, public policy and governance, military warfare & new technologies and traditional and non-traditional security related issues of global south region.
  • Policy Formulation Training: We also trained the young scholars regarding 'Policy Formulation, Policy Implementation and process of Policy Analysis' in the various disciplines such as politics, history, sociology, economy, industry, climate, foreign policy, health science & educational issues of global south region.
  • Research Associate / Assistant: Young aspirants can join 'Research Associate', or 'Research Assistant' to contribute in the research related studies and publish their findings on the Global South Observer platform.
  • Partnership & MOUs: Global South Observer is committed to increase our 'Partnership & MOUs' with academic & research institutions, industries, Universities and civil society groups and NGOs to encourage Global South related academic studies and new narratives.
  • Consultant Support: Global South Observer can also provide 'Consultant Support' to industry/MSMEs/start-ups, especially for major and minor projects associated with global south related issues.
